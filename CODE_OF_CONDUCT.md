# Code of Conduct

WetoLoop is an open-source engineering project. Participation should remain professional,
constructive and focused on improving the project.

## Expected behavior

- Be respectful when reviewing ideas, code and documentation.
- Critique technical decisions, not people.
- Provide enough context for others to reproduce or understand a problem.
- Accept that maintainers may reject changes that conflict with project scope or safety.
- Respect privacy, licensing and responsible disclosure requirements.

## Unacceptable behavior

Harassment, threats, discriminatory conduct, deliberate disruption, publication of private
information, or knowingly unsafe contributions are not acceptable.

## Enforcement

Maintainers may edit, hide or remove content and may restrict participation when necessary
to protect the project and its contributors. Serious concerns should be raised privately
when possible; security-sensitive reports must follow `SECURITY.md`.
